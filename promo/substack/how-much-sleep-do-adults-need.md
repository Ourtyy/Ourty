Sleep is often the first thing we give up when life gets busy. But it matters as much as food and exercise.

Health experts recommend that **adults get 7 or more hours a night** — yet around a third of us regularly get less.

**Three habits that make a real difference:**

- **Keep the same wake-up time**, even at weekends.
- **Put screens away 30–60 minutes before bed.**
- **Get daylight in the morning** to help set your body clock.

The full article explains why sleep matters, how to tell if your sleep quality is poor, and what to do if you can't fall asleep.
