Walk fast for three minutes, slow for three, repeat for half an hour. That's "Japanese walking", one of the most talked-about fitness trends of the year.

Unlike most viral workouts, this one comes from real research. Scientists at Shinshu University in Japan have studied interval walking for years. In their best-known trial, 246 adults with an average age of 63 tried it. The interval walkers gained more leg strength and fitness, and saw bigger drops in blood pressure, than people who walked at a steady pace.

**Three tips for your first session:**

- **Use the talk test.** On fast minutes, talking should feel like an effort. On slow minutes, you can chat easily.
- **Set a 3-minute timer** on your phone or watch so you don't have to count.
- **Start small.** Three or four rounds is a great beginning. Build up to five.

The full article covers what the research does and doesn't show (it's not a longevity hack!) and a simple step-by-step first session. It also has tips for keeping it up on dark winter days.
