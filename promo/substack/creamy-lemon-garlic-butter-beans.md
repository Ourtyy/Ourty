Beans are having a big moment. On TikTok, #BeanTok has people proudly stirring pots of chickpeas, black beans and, above all, big soft butter beans. For once, a viral food trend is built on solid nutrition.

Most of us eat far less fibre than the 30g a day the NHS recommends, and beans are one of the cheapest, easiest ways to close that gap. Research summarised by Harvard links eating legumes a few times a week to better heart health and lower cholesterol.

**Three tips for getting the most from beans:**

- **Start slowly.** The "two cups a day" rule some creators push is a big jump. Build up over a few weeks so your gut can adjust.
- **Rinse canned beans.** A quick rinse removes the starchy liquid and some of the salt.
- **Mash a few for creaminess.** Crushing some of the beans in the pan turns stock into a silky sauce, no cream needed.
