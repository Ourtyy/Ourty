Brown sugar and banana have had a good run. Now oats are going savory: cooked in broth and topped with garlicky greens, a jammy egg and a drizzle of olive oil.

It sounds unusual, but it makes a lot of sense. Most adults eat only about 20 g of fibre a day instead of the recommended 30 g, and oats are one of the easiest ways to close that gap. Their soluble fibre, beta-glucan, may help you feel full for longer. And a savory bowl skips the sugar while adding protein and vegetables.

**Three tips for your first savory bowl:**

- **Use rolled oats.** They turn creamy in about five minutes.
- **Cook them in low-salt stock** instead of water for instant flavour.
- **Top generously.** Try an egg, greens, a few tomatoes and a pinch of chilli.
