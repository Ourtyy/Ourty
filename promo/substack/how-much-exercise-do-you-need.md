Good news: the official exercise recommendation is easier to reach than most people think — and you don't need a gym.

The World Health Organization suggests adults aim for **150–300 minutes of moderate activity a week**, plus muscle-strengthening on two days. That's roughly 22 minutes of brisk walking a day.

**Three things you might not know:**

- **Every minute counts.** The old "at least 10 minutes at a time" rule is gone.
- **Use the talk test.** If you can talk but not sing, that's moderate activity.
- **Strength work can be simple** — squats, carrying shopping bags or heavy gardening all count.
