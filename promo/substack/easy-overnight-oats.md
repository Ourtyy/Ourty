If your mornings are a rush, this one's for you.

Overnight oats take five minutes to make before bed — and breakfast is waiting for you when you wake up. No cooking, no pans to wash.

They're also an easy way to eat more fibre. The NHS recommends 30 g a day, but most adults only get around 20 g.

**Three quick tips from this week's article:**

- **Let fruit do the sweetening.** Frozen berries melt into a lovely sauce overnight.
- **Add a spoon of chia seeds** for an extra fibre boost.
- **Make two jars at once** — they keep in the fridge for up to 2 days.

In the full article you'll find the basic recipe, four flavour ideas (apple pie, berry boost, banana & peanut butter, tropical) and swaps for dairy-free and gluten-free diets.
