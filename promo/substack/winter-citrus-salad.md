Just as the days turn grey, the shops fill up with the brightest fruit of the year. Oranges, blood oranges and grapefruit are at their juiciest in winter, and they make the perfect antidote to heavy comfort food.

This salad puts them centre stage with ruby pomegranate seeds, soft massaged kale and crunchy roasted chickpeas. It's also a smart pairing: your body can't store vitamin C, so you need some every day, and vitamin C helps you absorb the iron in plant foods like greens and beans.

**Three tips for the best citrus salad:**

- **Keep it raw.** Heat and long cooking break down vitamin C, so a fresh salad keeps more of it.
- **Massage the kale.** One to two minutes with a little oil and salt makes it soft and sweet.
- **Add crunch last.** Toss in the chickpeas and nuts just before serving so they stay crisp.
