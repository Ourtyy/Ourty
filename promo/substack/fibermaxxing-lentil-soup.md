Chia on everything, beans in every meal, fibre grams logged like steps... "Fibermaxxing" has taken over TikTok.

For once, a viral food trend is pointing in a useful direction. Most of us eat far too little fibre: in the US, more than 90% of women and 97% of men fall short. Eating more is linked to a healthier heart and gut and steadier blood sugar. But "maxxing" can backfire if you overdo it too fast.

**Three tips for doing it the friendly way:**

- **Build up slowly** over a couple of weeks. Jumping straight to lots of fibre can leave you gassy and bloated.
- **Drink more water** as you add fibre.
- **Choose whole foods over powders.** Beans, lentils, oats, fruit and veg give you fibre plus a lot more.

The full article explains how much fibre you actually need and the easy swaps that add up. It also has a hearty one-pot lentil, white bean and vegetable soup with roughly 12 g of fibre per bowl, cheap and freezer-friendly.
