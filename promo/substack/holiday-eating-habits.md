Big dinners, office parties, cookie tins and "just one more" glass of something festive: the holidays are here. Most of us want to enjoy every bite, but we'd rather not start January feeling sluggish and promising ourselves a new diet.

Here's a surprise from the research: the average holiday weight gain is only about a pound, much less than people think. The catch is that it tends to stay with us year after year. So the goal isn't strict rules or skipping dessert. It's a few small habits that let you enjoy the food and the company.

**Three easy habits to try this season:**

- **Don't skip meals to "save up."** Arriving starving makes it much harder to choose what you really want.
- **Slow down and savour.** Eating more slowly helps you notice when you're satisfied.
- **Take a short walk after eating.** Even five minutes can help steady your blood sugar after a big meal.
