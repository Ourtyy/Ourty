TITLE: Is Full-Fat Dairy Bad for You?
SUBTITLE: A new 12-week study challenges decades of low-fat advice. Here's what it really found
LINK: https://ourtyy.github.io/Ourty/posts/full-fat-dairy-study/
IMAGE: use the main photo of the article (or content/pins/full-fat-dairy-study.png)

For decades we were told to pick skimmed milk and fat-free yogurt. Now a new study from the University of Toronto has people wondering whether whole milk and real cheese are back on the menu.

Researchers gave adults with overweight or obesity three servings of full-fat dairy a day for 12 weeks. Compared with a low-dairy diet, they saw no meaningful changes in weight or cholesterol, and blood pressure actually improved.

It's good news, but it isn't a free pass. The study was small, short and partly funded by the dairy industry, and saturated fat still matters for your heart.

**Three simple ways to enjoy dairy wisely:**

- **Go plain and fermented.** Natural yogurt, kefir and good cheese beat sugary dairy desserts.
- **Watch the cheese portions.** One serving is about the size of two dice.
- **Balance your fats.** If you enjoy full-fat dairy, get most of your other fats from olive oil, nuts and fish.

The full article explains what the study found, why it doesn't settle the debate, how much dairy different guidelines suggest, and who should check with a doctor first.
