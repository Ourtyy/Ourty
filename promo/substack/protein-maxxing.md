Protein pancakes, protein coffee, protein chips, even protein water. "Protein-maxxing" has taken over supermarket shelves and social media feeds. But is all that extra protein really doing you any good?

The surprising answer: most people already eat enough. Survey data shows many adults already hit the newer, higher guidelines without a single shake or bar. Some people can benefit from a little more, including adults over 40, people who exercise regularly and anyone losing weight. Even then, experts say strength training matters far more than extra grams.

**Three simple ways to get protein right:**

- **Know your range.** Multiply your weight in kilograms by 1.2–1.6 to get a rough daily target if you're active or over 40.
- **Choose good sources.** Beans, lentils, fish, poultry, eggs, yogurt and nuts beat processed meat and sugary protein snacks.
- **Pair it with strength training.** Lifting, squatting and carrying are what really keep your muscles strong.
