When stress hits, our breathing becomes fast and shallow — often without us noticing.

The good news: slowing your breath on purpose can help your body calm down. It's free, takes five minutes, and you can do it at your desk, on the bus or in bed.

**The basics:**

- **Breathe in gently through your nose** for a count of five.
- **Breathe out through your mouth** for a count of five.
- **Keep going for 3–5 minutes.** Start with a count of three if five feels hard.

The full article walks you through it step by step, with tips to turn it into a daily habit.
