Move over, kale. Cabbage is officially cool again — Pinterest even named a "Cabbage Crush" one of its big food trends for 2026.

And this is one trend worth joining. Cabbage costs very little, keeps for weeks in the fridge and is at its best right now. Half a cup has only about 14 calories but plenty of vitamin C, plus a good dose of fibre. Roast it in a hot oven and the edges turn golden, sweet and crispy, almost like a chip.

**Three tips for the crispiest wedges:**

- **Keep a little core on each wedge** so the leaves hold together.
- **Turn the oven up high** (220°C / 425°F). That's what gives you the caramelised edges.
- **Don't crowd the tray.** If wedges touch, they steam instead of roasting.
