Ourty is a small, independent website about everyday wellbeing: what we eat, how we move, how we sleep and how we look after our minds.

## Our promise

- **Plain English.** No jargon, no miracle cures, no fear-mongering.
- **Sources you can check.** Every health claim links to a trusted source such as the World Health Organization, national health services or peer-reviewed research.
- **Small, realistic steps.** We focus on habits real people can keep.

## How we write

Our articles are researched and drafted with the help of AI tools, then read, fact-checked and edited by a human editor before they are published. If you spot a mistake, please [tell us](../contact/) and we will fix it quickly.

## Not medical advice

Ourty is not a medical service. Please read our [medical disclaimer](../disclaimer/).
